   (tilt folds −1.15/+0.37/−1.53°) → cleared on apply. Auto-cal no longer solves the gains by
   default (`tracker.vision.autoCalGains`): 0.966…0.990 tilt gain fit within 0.04° rms.
4. **Drive speed is latched at the command** (`probe-speed-zoomchange.ts`: a 3 s drive started
   at tele and zoomed to wide moved 4.50°, all-tele 4.46°, all-wide 45.67°). The driver picked
   the byte from a stale zoom reading → overshoot whenever the lens moves during a slew/zoom.
   Now: lens position estimated at send time, faster table of reading/estimate, re-latch.
5. **Invented speed gears**: `speedTableAt` interpolated bytes the rows never measured (zoom
   1385 bytes 5-7 = 1.9-2.6°/s) → the head lurched at 4-5°/s at tele (THY73G clip). Now measured
   bytes only.
