   locked → now searches at the frame-time position (+ learned lead + correction).

## Open issues — investigate root causes (priority order)

1. **`lost-lock` flags on the latest passes** (EIN961, UAL817, SKW4655, UAL949, SWA1170
   at ~22:30Z): lock 57-70% but one ~6 s gap at tele each (flag rule: tele gap > 3 s,
   `tracker/src/passes/quality.ts`). Two causes:
   (a) build i deliberately holds the zoom through brief losses (sigma grows 0.1°/s) —
   check the clips: is the plane IN FRAME (undetected) or drifting OUT during those
   gaps? If in frame, the vision is missing a visible plane (see 4); if out, the growth
   rate is too slow for this geometry (it should come from data — re-lock offsets
   were 0.4-1.0° for 1-8 s gaps). Possibly the metric should count "plane out of
   frame", not "no detection".

   with no lock for its first 5.9 s. Zoom should start from the search framing on a
   target change (check `resetSetpoint` / ladder state in tracker/src/loop.ts).

   that Zoom Direct (it ACKs everything, executes ~99%) and the driver never re-sent
   a deduplicated zoom. Zoom delivery is now closed-loop (visca.ts `checkZoomDelivery`;
   journal line "zoom N not taken up … resent"). Probe: `scripts/probe-zoom-busy.ts`.
